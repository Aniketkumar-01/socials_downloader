System Setup Permission

          
OmniDownloader runs 100% on your PC without external websites

        

        
      


      

        

          Why is this setup needed?
          
High-resolution video streams (1080p, 4K) and MP3 audio are processed locally on your PC using FFmpeg. FFmpeg merges video and audio streams into single, high-quality playable MP4 files.

        


        

          Terminal command to be executed:
          

            winget install Gyan.FFmpeg
            
          

        


        

          We never execute terminal commands or open browser websites without your permission. You can choose to let the app install FFmpeg in the background, or do it yourself in your own terminal.
